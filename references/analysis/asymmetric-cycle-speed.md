# Unequal cyclic amplitudes still lose maximum speed initially

8 September 2026. **Bounded actual-flow result, submitted for independent
review.** This tests an explicit amplitude modification of the same cyclic
sine prototype used in the
[equal-amplitude transfer](periodic-cycle-transfer.md)
and [subsequent speed calculation](cycle-next-transfer.md).
It is not a new architecture, an infinite-stage obstruction or a global
Navier–Stokes claim. No DNS is used. ROOT, E-prime and FORCED-D remain OPEN.

For every fixed positive triple a,b,c, set R=a²+b²+c² and P=a²b²c².
There is a positive T_(a,b,c), independent of viscosity 0<=mu<=1, such that
the actual smooth periodic solution from

    U0=(a sin y,b sin z,c sin x), force=0,

satisfies

    ||U_mu(tau)||infinity²
       <= R-R mu tau-(4/5)P tau^4 < R,
                        0<tau<=T_(a,b,c).                   (1)

Thus changing these three fixed positive amplitudes does not produce an
early global maximum-speed amplifier. The conclusion concerns this
interval and observable. It does not exclude later growth, changed phase
functions or geometry, more initial modes, or useful transfer measured
by another norm. Constant phase offsets are covered by translation below.

In particular the fixed choice (a,b,c)=(1,1,2) gives

    ||U_mu(tau)||infinity² <= 6-6mu tau-(16/5)tau^4.

The short-time constants may depend on the chosen amplitudes. There is
no assertion uniform as an amplitude tends to zero or infinity.

## 1. Complete first and second jets, including pressure

Use the torus of side 2pi and normalized equation

    U_tau+(U.grad)U+grad Pi=mu Delta U, div U=0.

Pressure has zero mean. Write U_E for the smooth auxiliary Euler
solution with the same datum. The exact initial nonlinear term is

    N=(ab sin z cos y,bc sin x cos z,ca sin y cos x).

Its divergence is zero. Therefore Pi_E(0)=0 and U_(E,1)=-N. A second
differentiation, with the pressure Poisson equation retained, gives

    Pi_(E,1)=-2abc cos x cos y cos z,
    U_(E,2)=T
      =-(ab² sin y sin²z,bc² sin z sin²x,ca² sin x sin²y).     (2)

Subscripts after the comma denote time derivatives at zero. Define

    F_abc = a sin x sin y cos y cos z
          + b sin y sin z cos x cos z
          + c sin x sin z cos x cos y.

The full next pressure jet is

    Pi_(E,2)=-(4/3)abc F_abc.                                (3)

For verification, with A_j=grad U_(E,j),

    2tr(A_1²+A_0 A_2)=-8abc F_abc,
    Delta Pi_(E,2)=+8abc F_abc.

Each term of F_abc has squared frequency 6. Equations
(2)–(3) include the generated reverse wave and its subsequent interaction;
the reverse amplitude is not independently supplied or held fixed.

Higher jets in this note are computed by the exact finite Fourier
recurrence of the equal-amplitude speed note. With Uhat_j(k)=i v_j(k),

    R_n(m)=sum_(j=0)^n binomial(n,j)
                  sum_(k+l=m) (l.v_j(k)) v_(n-j)(l),
    v_(n+1)(m)=R_n(m)-m(m.R_n(m))/|m|²,
    Pihat_n(m)=(m.R_n(m))/|m|²,                 m!=0.         (4)

Zero velocity mean is preserved and the pressure zero mode is fixed.
The [symbolic control](checks/check-asymmetric-cycle-speed.py)
uses arbitrary real symbols a,b,c, exact rational coefficients and every
mode generated by (4). The symbolic velocity mode counts through fourth
derivative are 6,12,18,48,98. At equal amplitudes eight fourth-derivative
modes cancel, giving the earlier count 90. Those modes are retained here
before specialization. This is exact differentiation, not a truncated
time-evolution model or DNS.

## 2. Moving maxima: unequal Hessians introduce no lower-order gain

Let G_E=|U_E|². Initially its maximum R occurs at

    p_sigma=(pi/2)(sigma1,sigma2,sigma3), sigma_j in {-1,1}.
    v_sigma=U0(p_sigma)=(a sigma2,b sigma3,c sigma1).
    D=diag(c²,a²,b²).

All eight maxima are nondegenerate for fixed positive a,b,c. Write
G_j=partial_tau^j G_E(0). Then

    G0=|U0|²,
    G1=-2U0.N,
    G2=2|N|²+2U0.T.

At each initial maximum p, with v=v_sigma, direct differentiation gives

    grad G0=0, Hess G0=-2D, D³G0=0,
    grad G1=2D v, Hess G1=0, grad G2=0.                       (5)

Consequently the nearby maximizing branch has

    p(tau)=p+tau v+O_(a,b,c)(tau³).                          (6)

The inverse Hessian in the first displacement cancels D exactly.
The quadratic displacement is zero. Thus no amplitude denominator
enters the fourth-order value through spatial optimization. Bounds on
the subsequent remainders may depend on the inverse of min(a²,b²,c²).

This resolves the misleading second-order comparison. At fixed p,
G_E has quadratic coefficient -(a²b²+b²c²+c²a²); moving the maximizing
point cancels it. Conversely, freezing just the initially generated
reverse wave in U0-tau N would predict the spurious optimized value

    R+(a²b²+b²c²+c²a²)tau²+O(tau³).

The complete nonlinear and pressure response (2) removes that apparent
amplification. For (1,1,2), the false quadratic gain would be 9tau².

## 3. Exact symmetry controls all maximum branches

No analyticity of the maximum of unrelated branches is assumed.
The datum has a symmetry that remains valid for unequal amplitudes.

Take any signs e1,e2,e3, set E=diag(e1,e2,e3), and choose shifts
delta_j in {0,pi} with

    cos(delta_j)=e_(j-1)e_j, indices cyclic modulo 3.

For the torus isometry T_e x=E x+delta, one has exactly

    U0(T_e x)=E U0(x).

Indeed component i depends only on x_(i+1), and
e_(i+1) cos(delta_(i+1))=e_i. Orthogonal invariance, translation
invariance and smooth uniqueness preserve the same identity for the
actual Euler and ordinary-viscosity NS solutions. The positive corner
is mapped to the corner with signs (e3,e1,e2), so all eight occur.

Hence |U|² takes equal values at the corresponding nearby maximizing
branches. A compactness gap excludes all points outside fixed small
neighborhoods of the initial maxima for sufficiently short time. The
negative Hessians persist within those neighborhoods, giving unique
nearby local maxima. The global maximum is therefore represented by
these symmetry-related smooth branches. There is no unresolved cubic
or quartic split between them.

Constant independent phase offsets do not change this conclusion. The
datum (a sin(y+alpha),b sin(z+beta),c sin(x+gamma)) is the original
datum translated by (gamma,alpha,beta). Translation invariance and
smooth uniqueness preserve its entire maximum-speed history. Thus all
such offsets are covered by the same bound, with the same constants;
they do not repair the early speed loss.

## 4. Full subsequent pressure gives a negative quartic coefficient

Define at each corner

    H_sigma=(ab²c² sigma2,bc²a² sigma3,ca²b² sigma1).

Exact evaluation of (4) along p+tau v yields

    U_E(tau,p+tau v)
       =v-(4/15)tau^4 H_sigma+O_(a,b,c)(tau^5).              (7)

All three lower positive-time coefficients on this line vanish.
The pressure contributions to order tau³ in its gradient are

| Pressure term | Coefficient of tau³ along p+tau v |
|---|---|
| tau Pi_(E,1) | 2H_sigma |
| (tau²/2) Pi_(E,2) | -(4/3)H_sigma |
| (tau³/6) Pi_(E,3) | (2/5)H_sigma |

Their sum is (16/15)H_sigma. In particular
grad Pi_(E,3)(p)=(12/5)H_sigma. This includes all the generated modes
in the full periodic pressure inverse. It agrees with the material
acceleration giving the coefficient -(4/15)H_sigma in (7).

By (5), grad G_E(tau,p+tau v)=O(tau³); by (6) the true maximizer is
O(tau³) away. Their G_E values therefore differ by O(tau^6).
Since v.H_sigma=3a²b²c², (7) and the global branch argument give

    M_E(tau):=||U_E(tau)||infinity²
      =R-(8/5)a²b²c² tau^4+O_(a,b,c)(tau^5).                (8)

This confirms the negative coefficient for every fixed positive triple,
not just numerical samples.

There is an independent structural check. The quartic value calculated
on p+tau v is a homogeneous polynomial of degree six in the amplitudes.
Changing any amplitude's sign is a spatial half-period translation;
the global coefficient is consequently even in each amplitude. If one
amplitude vanishes, the remaining Euler system has an exact triangular
completion preserving maximum speed. For example, when c=0,

    U_E(t,x,y,z)=(a sin(y-bt sin z), b sin z, 0), Pi=0.

The other cases follow by cyclic relabeling. Thus the quartic polynomial
vanishes on each coordinate amplitude plane and is divisible by a²b²c².
Its degree forces a constant multiple of that product. The independently
checked equal-amplitude value fixes the constant to -8/5. The direct
symbolic calculation (4),(7) also verifies this conclusion without relying
only on the structural argument.

## 5. Actual positive-time scope and positive viscosity

Fix a,b,c>0. The H20 local energy argument from the equal-amplitude
speed note applies to this fixed trigonometric datum with constants
depending on its amplitudes. Uniformly for 0<=mu<=1, there are
T0>0 and finite M such that

    sup_[0,T0] ||U_mu||H20 <= M,
    sup_[0,T0] ||partial_tau^j U_mu||H^(20-2j) <= C_j,
                                                     0<=j<=5. (9)

The minimum space is H10, which controls all spatial derivatives used
above. Taylor's integral remainder and the nondegenerate-maximum
argument give a constant C_E with the remainder in (8) bounded by
C_E tau^5. The full solution after time zero contains all its modes;
(9), not a finite Fourier closure, controls the remainder.

For W=U_mu-U_E, the exact difference equation retains the complete
projection and ordinary diffusion:

    W_tau+P[(U_E.grad)W+(W.grad)U_E+(W.grad)W]
                 -mu Delta W=mu Delta U_E, W(0)=0.

An H16 difference estimate using (9) gives ||W||H16<=C mu tau.
Its equation in H14 then gives
||W_tau-mu Delta U0||H14<=C mu tau. Since Delta U0=-U0,

    U_mu=U_E-mu tau U0+O_C2(mu tau²).                        (10)

This estimate resolves viscosity to first time order. A mere O(mu)
difference would not suffice. As U_E-U0=O_C2(tau),

    |U_mu|²=(1-2mu tau)|U_E|²+O_C0(mu tau²).

The multiplier is positive for tau<=1/4. Taking the global maximum
therefore gives

    M_mu(tau)=R-2R mu tau-(8/5)P tau^4
                     +O_(a,b,c)(tau^5+mu tau²).             (11)

There is no need to locate a different viscous maximizing branch.
For fixed constants C_E,C_V bounding the remainder, choose a positive
common time smaller than the local geometric and solution lifespans and

    T_(a,b,c) <= min(1/4, 4P/[5(1+C_E)], R/[1+C_V]).

The two error contributions are then at most (4/5)P tau^4 and
R mu tau, respectively. This proves (1) throughout that interval.
The constants are analytic local-estimate constants, not optimized
numerical thresholds.

## 6. What the result does and does not decide

For the explicit asymmetric datum (sin y,sin z,2 sin x), the full
nonlinear feedback gives

    M_E(tau)=6-(32/5)tau^4+O(tau^5),

and the strictly viscous conclusion (1) follows on a fixed short
interval. In physical variables u(t,x)=A U_mu(Aqt,qx), with integer q,
the viscosity parameter is mu=nu q/A. For any fixed nu>0 choose
A>=nu q. Speed squared acquires A² and the physical interval length
is T_(a,b,c)/(Aq). The input amplitudes and this scaling are explicit.

This excludes one attempted repair of the equal-cycle velocity-gain
requirement: fixed unequal positive strengths in these same three
modes do not create an early maximum-speed gain. It does not exclude
later growth of the same solution, differing frequency ratios, changed
phase functions or geometry, additional modes, or other gain observables.
Constant independent phase offsets are already covered by section 3.
The zero-amplitude limit
and h-dependent amplitude choices require separate uniform estimates;
the time constants here are not uniform in those limits.

No finite-time singularity, terminal force sequence or one-datum
infinite transfer is inferred. The exact symbolic controls check the
displayed finite algebra, symmetries and margins. The local existence,
global-maximizer and uniform-viscosity remainder are separate written
analytic arguments, subject to independent review.
